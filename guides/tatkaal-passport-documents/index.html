Tatkaal Passport Documents: What You Actually Need at the PSK

Tatkaal is not a different passport. It is a faster lane through the same Passport Seva system — and the documentary bar at the counter is higher, which is where most Tatkaal applications come unstuck.

Written for applicants in Taloja, Panvel and Navi Mumbai · Last checked 18 November 2026

What Tatkaal changes, and what it does not

Tatkaal is an out-of-turn processing scheme. You apply on the same Passport Seva portal, attend the same Passport Seva Kendra, and receive the same passport book. What you are paying for is priority.

Two points are widely misunderstood:

  • You do not need to prove urgency. The scheme does not require a reason, a ticket, or an emergency letter. Anyone eligible may use it.
  • Police verification is not skipped. For Tatkaal it generally moves to after the passport is issued rather than before. The verification still happens.

The document count

This is the part that differs most from a normal application, and the part people get wrong at the counter.

Applicants aged 18 and over

You must produce any three documents from Passport Seva's prescribed list of acceptable documents — plus your address proof, date-of-birth proof and non-ECR proof where those apply. The three Tatkaal documents are in addition to, not instead of, the usual proofs.

Applicants under 18

Minors must produce any two documents, drawn specifically from the first six entries on that list, again alongside the usual address, birth and non-ECR proofs.

The prescribed list runs to roughly thirteen items and includes documents such as Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, driving licence, birth certificate, bank or post office passbooks, property documents, ration card and service identity cards. Passport Seva publishes the authoritative list and an undertaking form on its Tatkaal page.

Aadhaar format matters. Passport Seva accepts a PVC Aadhaar card, the complete original Aadhaar letter, or an e-Aadhaar carrying the verified digital signature mark. Small cut-out Aadhaar cards and smart cards printed by non-UIDAI vendors are not accepted — a common and avoidable counter rejection.

Originals and photocopies

Carry original documents plus one set of self-attested photocopies of each to the PSK. Self-attested means you sign the copy yourself. Arriving with copies alone, or with originals but no copies, is one of the most frequent causes of a wasted appointment.

Who cannot use Tatkaal

Passport Seva maintains a list of excluded categories. Broadly, Tatkaal is not available where identity or citizenship needs closer scrutiny — for example applicants with a major change of name without the supporting gazette notification, applicants whose passport has been impounded or revoked, those with pending criminal proceedings, and several categories tied to citizenship by registration or naturalisation.

If any of this describes your case, confirm your eligibility before paying the Tatkaal premium, because the fee follows the application rather than the outcome.

How fast is it, really

The Passport Seva Citizen's Charter sets a target of issue within a small number of working days for an eligible Tatkaal application, against a longer window for the normal channel. These are service targets, not guarantees: an appointment slot still has to be available, your documents still have to clear the counter, and dispatch time depends on the post.

The practical bottleneck in Navi Mumbai is usually the appointment slot, not the processing. If your travel date is tight, check slot availability before you commit to Tatkaal — a normal application with an earlier slot sometimes lands sooner than a Tatkaal one three weeks out.

Before your appointment

  1. Confirm you are not in an excluded category.
  2. Pick your three documents (two for a minor) and check every detail matches across them — name spelling, father's name, date of birth.
  3. Add address, date-of-birth and non-ECR proofs as applicable.
  4. Make self-attested photocopies of everything and carry the originals too.
  5. Re-read the current requirements on the Passport Seva portal; the list and fees are revised from time to time.

Official sources

  • Passport Seva portal, Ministry of External Affairs
  • Documents Required for Tatkaal Application, Passport Seva

Document lists, fees and timelines are revised periodically. Verify current requirements on the Passport Seva portal before applying — it is the only authoritative source.
