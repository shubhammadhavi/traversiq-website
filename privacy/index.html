2. Privacy & Personal Document Handling Policy (DPDP Act Compliance)

Data Collected

When you enquire or engage our services, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and—only after engagement—documents required for your specific visa, passport, or travel filing.

Purpose & Access

Your personal documents are accessed solely by Shubham Madhavi for the purpose of drafting your application forms, verifying eligibility, or booking flights/hotels requested by you. We never sell or share your data with unauthorized third parties or marketers.

Document Retention & Deletion

Client documents shared via WhatsApp or email are retained only for the duration of your active application or travel itinerary. Upon completion of the service (or at any time upon your written request to shubhammadhavi9@gmail.com), your sensitive identity and financial documents are permanently deleted from our working devices.

3. Strict Document Integrity Policy

Traversiq Global assists strictly with organizing and presenting genuine, verifiable applicant documents. We do not manufacture, alter, or inflate bank statements, ITRs, employment letters, or academic records under any circumstances. Applicants are solely responsible for the authenticity of all documents submitted to embassies or passport authorities.
