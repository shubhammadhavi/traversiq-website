US F1 Student & B1/B2 Visitor Preparation

Honest, structured preparation for your US Consulate interview.

A US visa interview at the Mumbai BKC Consulate typically lasts under two minutes. Refusals under Section 214(b) frequently occur when the DS-160 contains incomplete one-line descriptions, omits mandatory disclosures, or when spoken answers sound memorized.

We work with you to ensure your DS-160, genuine financial documents, and spoken answers are clear and consistent. Strict Integrity Policy: We only organize and present genuine, verifiable applicant documents—we never fabricate, inflate, or alter financial or employment records.

Line-by-Line DS-160 & Social Media Compliance

Consular officers review your DS-160 before asking the first question.
